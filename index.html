✦ Our Story

Born from love, one sleepless night at a time

Tyler and Maddie met in college in 2015 and have been in love ever since. Last July, they welcomed their beautiful daughter, Adelyn — and their world changed forever.

Maddie has had the privilege of exclusively breastfeeding for 11 months, but faced one problem every single night she went to bed: being cold while co-sleeping. Too afraid to use a blanket near the baby, she spent hundreds of dollars on countless outfits trying to stay warm — none of them working quite right.

So Maddie did what mamas do best — she solved it herself. The Sleepy Family was born so that every co-sleeping mama could sleep warm, nurse easily, and keep their baby safe — all night long.

🌙

"For every mama who has ever been cold, tired, and just trying to do right by her baby — this one's for you."

— Tyler & Maddie
